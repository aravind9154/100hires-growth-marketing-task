# Sources

## Research Topic

AI-Powered SEO Content Production

## Objective

This research project focuses on identifying and analyzing leading experts who actively publish insights on AI-powered SEO content production. The collected research will be used to understand modern content workflows, semantic SEO, topical authority, AI-assisted content creation, search quality, and content scaling strategies.

## Expert Selection Criteria

* Active practitioners rather than commentators
* Consistently publishing on SEO, AI, and content production
* Demonstrated expertise through research, experimentation, and implementation
* Strong reputation within the SEO industry
* High-signal sources suitable for building a practical AI-powered SEO playbook

## Research Scope

The research will include:

* LinkedIn posts
* YouTube transcripts
* Blog articles
* Newsletters
* Podcast appearances
* Other publicly available educational content
