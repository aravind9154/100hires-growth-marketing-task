   This helps distinguish performance in AI experiences from performance in standard web results.

4. The update creates new opportunities for advanced SEO analysis.

- AI visibility is becoming a measurable SEO performance category.
- Separating AI impressions from traditional search impressions enables deeper performance analysis.
- Technical, semantic, and behavioral signals may all contribute to AI selection.
- AI search optimization requires understanding not only visibility but also the factors driving inclusion.

### Strategic Interpretation

- SEO teams can use AI performance data to identify patterns that influence AI selection and citation.
- Future competitive advantage may come from understanding AI inclusion signals better than competitors.
- Success metrics should evolve from ranking-focused analysis toward AI visibility and selection analysis.

## Post 2 

Title : Long-term Programmatic SEO Strategy

URL : https://www.linkedin.com/posts/koray-tugberk-gubur_an-example-of-a-long-term-programmatic-seo-activity-7467294146513305600-W-hq
  
Publication date : 2 weeks ago

### Key Highlights

1. A long-term programmatic SEO project successfully benefited from the May 2026 BCAU (Broad Core Algorithm Update).
   The project demonstrated how sustained improvements can eventually lead to significant ranking gains.

2. Website performance is influenced by both official and unofficial Google re-ranking events.
   Important ranking changes can occur outside publicly announced update dates.

3. Sites affected by similar update patterns may belong to the same algorithmic classification group.
   Shared ranking movements can reveal how search engines categorize websites.

4. The project exhibited both positive and negative ranking signals over time.
   This created an "arch" ranking pattern before the eventual breakthrough.

5. Ranking State plays a critical role in search performance.
   Websites often remain within specific ranking ranges until the search engine changes its assessment.

6. Some websites face ranking ceilings despite ongoing optimization.
   Significant growth may only occur when the site's Ranking State changes.

7. Ranking gains are not always permanent.
   If improved rankings are not supported by user satisfaction and engagement, future re-rankings may reverse the gains.

8. Structural changes contributed to recovery and growth.
   Moving content into subdomains helped improve performance after previous HCU-related impacts.

9. Functional website features created additional value.
   Adding "buy" and "compare" functionality made the site more useful for users.

10. Google's systems increasingly evaluate purpose and utility.
    Websites that demonstrate real brand value, functionality, and user usefulness appear more likely to succeed.

### Key Insights

- Search engines classify websites into ranking states that influence their performance limits.
- User utility and functionality are becoming as important as content quality.
- Algorithmic reclassifications can create major ranking shifts beyond official update announcements.
- Sustainable rankings require both visibility and positive user experience signals.

### Strategic Interpretation

- SEO success increasingly depends on building websites with genuine utility rather than content-only strategies.
- Organizations should monitor unofficial ranking shifts alongside official Google updates.
- Improving user outcomes can help trigger favorable algorithmic reclassification over time.
- Long-term SEO growth may require changing how search engines perceive the site's overall purpose and value.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

- AI-generated content should be integrated into functional user experiences rather than existing as standalone informational pages.
- Content strategies should support actions such as comparison, evaluation, decision-making, and purchasing.
- AI-powered content production should strengthen brand identity, purpose, and usefulness across the website.
- Success in AI-driven search environments will likely depend on combining high-quality content with practical functionality, strong user engagement, and clear business value.

## Post 3 

Title : Google AI Content Guidelines Update

URL : https://www.linkedin.com/posts/koray-tugberk-gubur_google-has-updated-its-guidelines-for-ai-activity-7462218037690073088-8Hz5
  
Published date : 1 month ago

### Key Highlights

1. Google’s updated AI Search guidance reinforces that AI search optimization is fundamentally part of SEO.
   Concepts such as AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), and similar labels are not separate disciplines.

2. Traditional ranking signals remain essential for AI visibility.
   If a page cannot rank in search results, its content is less likely to be surfaced or cited in AI-generated answers.

3. Passage-level visibility depends on page-level authority.
   Strong passages typically originate from pages that already demonstrate ranking potential and trustworthiness.

4. Google's "query fan-out" explanation supports the concept of query augmentation.
   AI systems expand and refine queries to retrieve relevant information from multiple sources.

5. AI retrieval mechanisms are closely aligned with established search principles.
   The process relies on finding, filtering, and selecting trustworthy documents based on relevance and authority.

6. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) functions as an answer-generation layer built on search retrieval.
   It gathers information from indexed documents before synthesizing responses.

7. Incomplete or ambiguous queries trigger broader retrieval processes.
   AI systems may retrieve a larger set of documents when user intent is unclear.

8. Traditional ranking signals remain influential in AI systems.
   Factors such as PageRank, topical authority, and consensus continue to influence source selection.

9. The principles behind AI answer selection have deep roots in search research.
   Concepts such as answer prominence, corroboration, and answer scoring remain relevant today.

10. Advanced AI visibility strategies are extensions of SEO rather than replacements for it.
    Techniques involving additional domains, consensus building, and authority development are still forms of SEO implementation.

### Key Insights

- AI search optimization builds upon traditional SEO rather than replacing it.
- Retrieval systems rely heavily on authority, relevance, and consensus signals.
- Strong page-level rankings improve the likelihood of AI citations and passage selection.
- Many AI search concepts are evolutions of established search-engine principles rather than entirely new methodologies.

### Strategic Interpretation

- Organizations should strengthen core SEO foundations before investing heavily in specialized AI optimization tactics.
- Building topical authority and trust remains critical for both traditional and AI-powered search visibility.
- SEO teams should view AI search as an extension of search ecosystems rather than a separate marketing channel.
- Long-term competitive advantage comes from improving authority, consensus, and retrieval relevance rather than chasing new terminology.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

- AI-generated content should be designed to rank well in traditional search because ranking signals influence AI retrieval and citation.
- Content production should focus on building topical authority through comprehensive topic coverage, entity relationships, and semantic relevance.
- Content should be structured for both document-level authority and passage-level retrievability, making important information easy for AI systems to extract.
- AI-powered content workflows should prioritize trust, accuracy, corroboration, and consensus-building signals to improve both search rankings and AI citation potential.
