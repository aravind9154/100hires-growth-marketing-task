# Research Methodology

## Overview

This document describes the methodology used to build a structured dataset on **AI-powered SEO and modern growth marketing practices**.

The goal of this research is to collect, organize, and analyze real-world content from active practitioners in order to extract repeatable patterns and strategic insights.

---

## Research Objective

The primary objectives of this research are:

- Understand how AI is transforming SEO workflows
- Analyze content creation and distribution strategies used by growth marketers
- Identify repeatable frameworks used across LinkedIn and YouTube
- Build a structured dataset for future insight extraction and playbook creation

---

## Expert Selection Criteria

A total of 10 experts were selected based on the following criteria:

- Active practitioners in SEO, AI content, or growth marketing
- Consistent publishing of high-quality, actionable content
- Demonstrated real-world execution experience (not purely theoretical writing)
- Strong presence on LinkedIn and/or YouTube
- Relevance to modern AI-driven search and content systems

Only experts meeting multiple criteria were included to ensure dataset quality and relevance.

---

## Data Sources

The dataset is built using multiple content sources:

- LinkedIn posts (primary source for short-form insights and strategy updates)
- YouTube video transcripts (long-form educational content and frameworks)
- Supporting materials such as blogs, case studies, and reports

Each source is stored separately in structured folders for easier analysis.

---

## Data Collection Process

The following process was used to collect data:

1. Identify relevant experts based on selection criteria
2. Extract LinkedIn posts manually or via scraping tools
3. Fetch YouTube transcripts using API-based or automated scripts
4. Store raw data in structured folders by author and content type
5. Standardize file naming for consistency and traceability

---

## Data Processing Pipeline

Collected data follows this pipeline: Collection → Cleaning → Structuring → Organization → Analysis Preparation


### Processing Steps:
- Remove irrelevant or duplicate content
- Standardize formatting of posts and transcripts
- Organize data by expert and platform
- Tag content based on topic relevance (SEO, AI, growth strategy)

---

## Quality Control

To ensure dataset reliability:

- Only verified experts were included
- Duplicate or low-quality content was filtered out
- Content unrelated to SEO or growth marketing was excluded
- Recent and relevant materials were prioritized

---

## Analysis Framework

The collected dataset is designed to support structured analysis in the following areas:

- AI-driven SEO strategy patterns
- Content creation frameworks used by top practitioners
- LinkedIn and YouTube distribution strategies
- Engagement hooks and messaging patterns
- Automation and scaling techniques in content workflows

---

## Tools Used

- Python scripts for transcript extraction and automation
- Manual curation for LinkedIn content collection
- GitHub for structured storage and version control

---

## Expected Outcome

The final outcome of this research is a structured knowledge base that can be used to:

- Build a practical AI SEO playbook
- Identify high-performing growth marketing strategies
- Understand modern content distribution systems
- Support future AI-driven marketing experiments

---

## Summary

This methodology ensures that the dataset is:

- Structured
- Reproducible
- Filtered for quality
- Aligned with real-world AI SEO practices

It forms the foundation for extracting actionable insights from practitioner-led content.
