# AI-Powered SEO Content Production Playbook

## Overview

This directory contains the final playbook created from research conducted on 10 SEO experts specializing in AI-powered SEO content production.

The playbook transforms the research findings into a practical Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for planning, creating, optimizing, publishing, and maintaining AI-assisted SEO content. It also documents areas where experts disagree, explains the decisions I made during the research process, presents one original idea, discusses the limitations of the playbook, and identifies which expert I would be least likely to recommend for this topic.
